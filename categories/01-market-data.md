### yfinance

- Link: https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance
- Category: Market Data / Python Library
- License: Apache-2.0
- Use Case: Download historical market data from Yahoo Finance for research, analysis, and prototyping.
- Strengths: Easy to use, widely adopted, suitable for quick financial data experiments.
- Limitations: Unofficial Yahoo Finance interface; data availability and reliability may change.
- Relevance to Specularis: Useful for quick prototypes, market dashboards, and research scripts before connecting to more stable paid data sources.
- Notes: Best used for research and non-critical workflows, not as the only source for live trading decisions.
